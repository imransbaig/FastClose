We previously sent you a CP2000 notice proposing changes to your 2024 Form 1040, and we didn't receive a response resolving the issue. This is your Notice of Deficiency under IRC section 6212. We have determined a deficiency (increase in tax) of $5,732.25 for tax year 2024, plus an accuracy-related penalty of $1,146.45 under IRC section 6662, for a total of $6,878.70. What you need to do
If you want to challenge this determination in court before paying, you must file a petition with the United States Tax Court no later than 90 days from the date of this notice. The court can't consider a late petition, and we can't extend this time. You can file the petition online at www.ustaxcourt.gov or mail it to the court at 400 Second Street NW, Washington, DC 20217.

If you agree, sign and return the enclosed Form 5564, Notice of Deficiency - Waiver, so we can assess the tax and send you a bill. If you neither petition the Tax Court nor sign the waiver, we will assess the deficiency after the petition period ends.

Visit www.irs.gov/cp3219a for more information about this notice. Keep this notice with your 2024 tax records.
