We're reviewing your 2025 tax return

We're holding your refund until we finish reviewing your 2025 Form 1040. We're verifying the income, income tax withholding, tax credits, and business income you reported. Returns are selected for this review based on the information we receive; being selected doesn't mean you made an error.

What you need to do
You don't need to do anything at this time, and you don't need to send us any documents or call us. We will contact you if we need additional information. Please don't file a second return or an amended return; doing so will delay the review.

What happens next
If our review shows no changes, we will send your refund. If we need information, we will send you a letter telling you exactly what to send. We expect to complete the review within 60 days of the date of this notice. If you haven't received your refund or a letter after 60 days, you may call us at 800-829-1040 with this notice available. No payment is requested with this notice.

Visit www.irs.gov/cp05 for more information about this notice. Keep this notice with your 2025 tax records.
