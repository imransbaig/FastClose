We're auditing the Earned Income Credit (EIC) and the dependents claimed on your 2025 Form 1040. We're holding the portion of your refund related to these items, $5,959.42, until we complete the audit. The rest of your refund has been released.

What you need to do
Send us copies of documents by the due date, which is 30 days from the notice date that show each child you claimed lived with you in the United States for more than half of 2025, such as school records, medical records, or a letter from a landlord or childcare provider on official letterhead showing the child's name, your address, and the dates covered. Also send proof of your relationship to each child, such as a birth certificate. Use the enclosed Form 886-H-EIC as a checklist. Send copies, not originals, in the enclosed envelope or fax them to 855-555-0147.

If you don't send the documents within 30 days from the date of this notice, we will disallow the credit, and you may owe additional tax, penalties, and interest. If we find the claim was made recklessly, you may be banned from claiming the EIC for 2 years; 10 years if fraudulent.
