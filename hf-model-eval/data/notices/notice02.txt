Information doesn't match your 2024 tax return

We received information from third parties about income or payments that doesn't match what you reported on your 2024 Form 1040. We haven't proposed any changes to your tax yet. This notice asks you to explain the difference before we do.

Information reported to us that isn't on your return
  SILVERLINE CONSULTING GROUP INC, Form 1099-NEC (nonemployee compensation): $30,373.53

What you need to do
Respond by the due date, which is 30 days from the notice date. Complete the enclosed Response form, explain why the information above differs from your return, and attach copies of documents that support your explanation, such as a corrected Form 1099-NEC, your brokerage statements, or your general ledger. If you agree that the income was left off your return, tell us so on the Response form and we will send you a notice showing the additional tax, penalty, and interest you owe.

If you don't respond, we may propose changes to your return based on the information we received and send you a notice showing the amount you owe.

You don't need to send a payment with your response, and you don't need to file an amended return. Don't send original documents; we can't return them.

If you have questions, call us at 800-829-8310 and have this notice available.
