We need to verify your identity before we process your 2025 tax return

We received a federal income tax return, Form 1040, for tax year 2025 with your name and Social Security number. To protect you from identity theft, we need to verify that you filed this return before we process it. Until we hear from you, we can't process the return or issue any refund claimed on it.

What you need to do
Call the Taxpayer Protection Program at 800-830-5084 within 30 days from the date of this letter. When you call, have available: this letter; a copy of the 2025 tax return you filed, if you filed one; a copy of a prior-year tax return; and supporting documents such as Forms W-2 and 1099. We can't verify your identity online for this letter; you must call.

If you didn't file a 2025 tax return, call the same number so we can remove the return from your account and protect you from further misuse of your identity. You may also complete Form 14039, Identity Theft Affidavit.

If you don't call us no later than 30 days from the date of this letter, we won't be able to process the return, and any refund claimed will not be issued. This letter doesn't ask you to pay anything.

Visit www.irs.gov/identitytheft for more information about this letter. Keep this letter with your 2025 tax records.
