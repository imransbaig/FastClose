What you need to do
File the 2025 Form 1065 within 30 days of the date of this notice. You can file electronically through an authorized e-file provider or mail the return to the address at the top of this notice with a copy of this notice on top.

If you already filed, send us a signed copy of the return and proof of filing, such as the e-file acceptance acknowledgment, with the enclosed response page no later than 30 days from the date of this notice. If you don't respond, we may charge a penalty under IRC section 6698 for each month or part of a month the return is late, multiplied by the number of partners, and each partner's own return may be delayed or examined.
